# Project demo video — 2-minute script (editable)

Narration is read by a local Kokoro ONNX voice (af_heart, en-US,
24 kHz). Scenes 6-8 use tightened wording for pacing; scenes 1-5
are unchanged.

## Scene 1 · 0:00–0:12 — "When should an operator trust an AI alert?"

> An AI prediction is useful only when an operator knows when to trust it. This research demo brings uncertainty, evidence, and operational context into one decision console.

## Scene 2 · 0:12–0:27 — "Predict. Calibrate. Explain."

> Machine learning estimates equipment health and future electricity demand. Conformal calibration adds prediction intervals. The system then turns interval evidence into alerts and retrieves relevant procedures to support the operator.

## Scene 3 · 0:27–0:46 — "Marine: watch before acting"

> In the marine replay, a public gas turbine simulator provides the operating data. The blue band shows uncertainty around the health estimate. When that band crosses the action threshold, the agent raises a watch alert: gather evidence before committing to maintenance.

## Scene 4 · 0:46–1:02 — "Confirmed means stronger evidence"

> A confirmed alert requires the entire interval to pass the threshold. This reduces premature escalation, but can delay confirmation. The console keeps both watch and confirmed states visible, alongside the conventional point estimate alarms.

## Scene 5 · 1:02–1:16 — "Same evidence. Less overload."

> Operators do not always have time for a long explanation. Simulated alert pressure switches the advisory from a detailed view to a focused set of actions. The underlying prediction and alert decision stay the same.

## Scene 6 · 1:16–1:34 — "Energy: plan with uncertainty"

> The energy console forecasts Finnish residual load three hours ahead. Calibrated intervals support reserve planning, and rolling coverage charts monitor performance. Coverage applies across cases, not to every individual forecast.

## Scene 7 · 1:34–1:50 — "Optional LLM. Fixed numerical evidence."

> Optional GPT O S S writes explanations from retrieved knowledge. Numerical confidence stays computed by the app. Operators can inspect citations, and an offline composer takes over if the language model fails.

## Scene 8 · 1:50–2:00 — "From prediction to an informed decision"

> A research demonstration, not a production safety system. Public data and synthetic manuals support the next step: human review and validation with real operators.
