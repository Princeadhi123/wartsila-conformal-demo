## Background
Fleet experience with marine gas turbines shows compressor fouling accelerates in warm, humid, salt-laden air and near industrial coasts, and that a given vessel can foul two to three times faster in summer coastal trade than on open-water winter routes. Wash fluid choice and rinse quality materially affect recovery: detergent residue left in the gas path attracts fresh deposits and can shorten the interval to the next alert.

## Recommendations
Use only demineralised water for routine washes, and where detergent is used, always follow with a complete rinse. In high-fouling seasons, shorten the assessment interval and trend T2 and P2 weekly at a fixed load point. Do not extend maintenance intervals on the basis of a single good post-wash kMc reading; require two consistent steady-state measurements before revising the plan.
