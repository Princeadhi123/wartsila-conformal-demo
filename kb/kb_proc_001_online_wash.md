## Applicability
Online washing, performed while the gas turbine runs at reduced or normal load, removes loose surface deposits and slows kMc decline. It is a light maintenance action, not a substitute for offline washing, and only partial recovery should be expected. Use it for early decay, as a bridging measure before a port call, or as a routine preventive task in high-fouling seasons.

## Procedure steps
1. Confirm ambient temperature is above freezing and engine load is stable.
2. Prepare demineralised water, or the approved detergent solution where the engine manual specifies it.
3. Reduce to the wash speed or power setting recommended by the engine manual.
4. Inject wash fluid through the dedicated nozzles for the prescribed duration while monitoring T2, P2 and vibration.
5. Run a rinse pass with clean demineralised water.
6. Return to normal load and hold steady for at least 10 minutes before evaluating kMc.

## Post-wash evaluation
Log kMc before and after the wash at a comparable steady load point, not during transients, because a transient comparison exaggerates both apparent decay and apparent recovery. If recovery is less than about half of the accumulated decay, or if decay re-establishes within a few weeks, schedule an offline crank wash at the next practical opportunity and note the poor response in the maintenance record.
