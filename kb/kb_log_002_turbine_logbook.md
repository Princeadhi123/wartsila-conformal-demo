## 2025-02-11 - Vessel D: T48 rise, borescope found tip rub
A T48 rise of about 18 degrees at constant power with kMt 0.981, interval fully below threshold. Borescope inspection showed first-stage tip rub and light leading-edge erosion, consistent with a casing distortion event. The engine was de-rated by 7 percent, which restored T48 margin, and the inspection interval was shortened to 500 operating hours pending trend review.

The rub was attributed to transient differential expansion during a cold start.

## 2025-07-08 - Vessel E: hot corrosion after low-grade fuel period
A gradual kMt decline to 0.979 with rising fuel flow over six weeks. Borescope found coating loss and discoloration consistent with hot corrosion following a period on lower-grade fuel. A fuel quality review was initiated, bunkering records were checked, and the engine held at reduced rating pending shop-visit planning at the next major port window.

A check of sister vessels on the same fuel batch was also ordered.

## 2025-09-25 - Vessel D: stable findings, rating kept
The follow-up borescope showed no progression of the earlier tip rub, and the kMt trend was flat at 0.980 plus or minus 0.004 over the intervening months. The de-rate was kept as a conservative margin but no repair was triggered, which avoided an unnecessary hot-section opening and its cost.

The case was logged as an example of patience paying off when findings are stable.
