## When to de-rate
De-rate when confirmed kMt decay is below the action threshold, when T48 approaches its continuous limit at the required power, or when borescope findings show moderate-to-severe degradation with progression between inspections. De-rating is a protective operating limit, not a repair; it buys time to plan work properly and is preferable to running a degraded turbine at full rating.

Treat it as part of normal operations, not a failure.

## How to de-rate
Reduce the maximum lever position or power setting so that T48 stays below the continuous limit with margin, and document the new limit in the operating profile and alarm settings. A five-to-ten percent power reduction typically recovers several tens of degrees of T48 margin. Verify the margin at the highest expected ambient temperature, not only at trial conditions, and check it again whenever the operating profile changes.

## Return to service
Restore the full rating only after inspection confirms the findings are stable and, where applicable, after repair is completed. Re-baseline the kMt trend after any hot-section work, since the post-repair coefficient will differ from the historical decaying trend. Update the alert thresholds and the operator briefing notes so the next decay episode is judged against the right baseline.

Log the rating change in the vessel's operating-history file.
